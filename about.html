History

Founded in 2015 by former U.S. Army Captain David Molina, Molinas Construction Company ("Molinas") is a family-owned and operated federal contractor specializing in the procurement and supply of construction materials, equipment, and mission-critical resources for government customers.

What began in the family's garage with a $3,000 loan and a shoestring budget has evolved into a business supporting federal projects and sourcing materials and equipment globally.

Following his departure from active duty at Dover Air Force Base in 2013, David entered the federal contracting industry, drawing on his military experience and entrepreneurial background to build a company focused on supporting government missions.

Experience

Since 2015, Molinas has developed its capabilities through public works construction, project management, equipment operations, logistics, and the procurement of specialized materials and equipment for government customers.

Our first major public works project, Mill Creek Levee Vegetation Maintenance Phase I, established the company's foundation in project execution. Molinas provided executive oversight, project management, field supervision, quality assurance, and day-to-day foreman responsibilities, gaining firsthand experience in managing federal project requirements.

In 2017, Molinas was awarded a five-year Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract at Willamette National Cemetery, where our founder personally operated a Caterpillar 316E excavator to remove and load more than 3,000 cubic yards of spoils material annually.

Building on this operational foundation, Molinas expanded its capabilities to support general contractors and government agencies with construction materials, material-handling equipment, specialized equipment, and mission-critical procurement requirements for military personnel and other federal end users.

Across every engagement, Molinas emphasizes accountability, cooperation, operational efficiency, and mutual respect. We recognize that successful contract execution depends on understanding the end user's requirements, coordinating resources effectively, and delivering dependable results.

From supporting essential infrastructure to sourcing specialized equipment, Molinas remains committed to improving project delivery and supporting the government missions we serve.

Certifications

Molinas has participated in federal, state, and regional small business certification programs supporting veteran-owned, disadvantaged, minority-owned, and emerging small businesses.

The company has held Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) and HUBZone certifications, along with Oregon DBE, MBE, ESB, and veteran-owned business credentials (Certification No. 9876), and Washington veteran-owned business certification.

Current certification status and supporting documentation are available upon request.

Leadership

David Molina, Founder & CEO

David Molina founded Molinas Construction Company in 2015 following more than a decade of military service as an enlisted soldier and commissioned officer in the United States Army.

David enlisted as a private first class in 2000 and subsequently entered Army ROTC following September 11, 2001. He earned a degree in Political Science and received his commission as an Infantry officer in 2004.

Throughout his military career, David held leadership and operational assignments, including service with the 364th Civil Affairs Brigade, Aide-de-Camp to the Assistant Division Commander–Operations at the 104th Division, and assignments supporting casualty and mortuary affairs operations.

During his final active-duty assignment at Dover Air Force Base, David served as a Captain and S-2/3 Officer in Charge, responsible for plans, training, and operations in support of the Families of the Fallen mission.

His military decorations and recognitions include the Army Meritorious Service Medal, Army Commendation Medal, and Lieutenant Rowan Award.

Drawing on this experience, David founded Molinas to support government customers through responsive procurement, project management, supply-chain coordination, and contract execution.

As Founder and CEO, David oversees business development, government solicitations, supplier relationships, procurement, and delivery coordination. His approach emphasizes accountability, dependable execution, and understanding the operational requirements of the end user.

Rain or shine, David Molina will never ask anyone to do anything he isn't willing to do first. Pictured on one of Molinas' earliest public works projects (2015).

His board service includes the Oregon State University Alumni Association, Oregon Museum of Science and Industry, LatinoBuilt, American Society of Professional Estimators Columbia-Pacific Chapter, Oregon Community Foundation Latino Partnership Council, Oregon State University Board for Diversity and Inclusion, and Oregon Commission on Hispanic Affairs.
